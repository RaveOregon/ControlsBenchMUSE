Failure CP-4: when Motor_RunCmd was removed, Drive_RunningFB turned off as soon as the drive entered the stopping phase, but beltSpeedMps continued decaying gradually, so cartons and beltTravelM (hence Encoder_Count) kept moving while the drive was reported stopped. Fix: force belt speed, belt travel delta, and carton motion to zero on any tick where Drive_RunningFB is off, so physical motion, encoder progression, and running feedback agree; acceleration ramp-up while running is unchanged.
